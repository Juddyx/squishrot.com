A little help for your jellies.

Email support@squishrot.com and a person will get back to you. Include your iPhone model, the Squishrot version (at the bottom of Settings), what happened and roughly when. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or card details.

Coins or Pro haven't arrived

Connect to the internet and open Squishrot, signed in to the same account you bought with. A purchase that's still being checked finishes on its own when the app opens. Please don't buy the same pack again.

For Pro, tap Profile → Settings → Restore purchases, using the Apple Account you subscribed with. Coin packs that were already delivered and spent can't be restored. Still stuck? Email us with the product, roughly when you bought it and your Apple order number.

Refunds are handled by Apple. Follow Apple's refund steps.

Cancel Pro

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Squishrot, or follow Apple's steps. Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel it.

Get back into your account

Sign in the same way you did the first time. With email, ask for a new link and use the newest one, and check your spam folder. With Sign in with Apple, use the same Apple Account. If you chose Hide My Email, your account uses an Apple relay address.

No jellies on the map

Check that Squishrot can use your location while the app is open (iPhone Settings → Squishrot → Location) and that Precise Location is on. Then open the map with an internet connection and give it a moment to find you. Always stop somewhere safe before playing.

See how Squishrot uses your location.

Delete your account

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. This removes your profile, collection, location and game progress and closes your open trades. What's deleted and what's kept.

Can't sign in? Email support@squishrot.com from the address on your account and we'll help. We may need to check the account is yours first.

A problem with another player

Tap Report or Block on their listing, offer or Den. Reports are reviewed by a person within 24 hours. You can unblock players in Settings → Blocked collectors.

Never trade outside the app or for real money. To appeal a suspension, email support@squishrot.com.

For parents

Players under the age of digital consent in their country play Squishrot on their phone only: no account, no online trading and no coin shop, and a grown-up check before any purchase or outside link. More in our privacy policy.
