Your score

Four things add up

Your jellies

Every jelly you own adds points by its finish, from 90 for a Common to 1,800 for a Diamond.

Your XP

Opening drops, blending and playing earn XP, and all of it counts toward your score.

Trades

240 points for every trade you complete, whichever side of it you were on.

Your streak

55 points for each day of your current streak. Miss a day and the streak, with its points, starts over.

Seasons

A cup every month

Each calendar month is a season. When it ends, everyone on the board gets a cup for the place they finished in, cast in jelly and kept on their profile for good.

  • Champion1st
  • Runner-up2nd
  • Third place3rd
  • Top 104th–10th
  • Top 10011th–100th
  • FinisherEveryone else on the board

Seasons follow UTC months and cups are handed out early on the 1st. Your place for the month is the last one recorded: each night, and whenever you open the leaderboard.

How the board works

Fair, and the same for everyone

  1. One world board

    Collectors are ranked by score, and your place is the same whoever is looking. Equal scores share a place.

  2. Find it on your profile

    Your place shows on your Profile. Tap it to open the leaderboard and see who’s just above you.

  3. Meet the collectors

    Tap anyone on the board to see their profile and showcase, and visit their Den.

  4. Safe by design

    The board shows names, pictures and scores, never where anyone is. Players you block don’t appear for you.
