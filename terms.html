Playing Squishrot

Squishrot shows jellies on a map near you. Watch where you're going, stay out of places you shouldn't be, and never play while driving or crossing a road. You're responsible for your own safety while you play.

Your account

Keep your sign-in to yourself; you're responsible for what happens on your account. We may limit, suspend or close accounts that break these terms or the Community Rules, put other players at risk, or abuse the game's economy. If you think we got it wrong, email support@squishrot.com and a person will look at it again.

Jellies, coins and diamonds

Jellies, coins, diamonds and other in-game items are licensed to you for use in Squishrot. They aren't real money, can't be exchanged for money, and aren't your property. Rarity odds are shown in the app before you buy.

Purchases are made through Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple under its policies; if Apple refunds a purchase, the coins or Pro it gave are removed from your account.

Squishrot Pro

Squishrot Pro is a monthly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel at any time in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions, and Pro stays on until the end of the period you've paid for. Deleting the app or your account doesn't cancel it.

Trading

Trades happen only inside the app, and nothing moves until both players agree. Finished trades are final. We may block or reverse trades that are fraudulent, automated, copied through a bug, or part of a real-money deal. Never buy or sell jellies or coins for real money outside the app.

Community Rules

  • Be kind. No bullying, threats, hate, harassment or sexual content.
  • Keep names, bios and notes clean, and keep personal details like your phone number, address or social accounts out of them.
  • Trade fairly. No scams, fake offers or real-money deals.
  • Play fair. No bots, scripts, modified apps or exploiting bugs. If you find a bug, tell us.
  • Don't pretend to be someone else.

You can report or block any player from their listing, offer or Den. Reports are reviewed by a person within 24 hours, and anything or anyone breaking these rules is removed.
