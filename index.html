FAQ

Good questions

Is Squishrot free?

Yes. Squishrot is free to download and play. Coin packs and Squishrot Pro are optional in-app purchases.

Do I have to walk around to play?

Drops appear around wherever you are, so you can play anywhere. Walk within 80 m of a drop and hold it to open it. Pro stretches your reach to 150 m.

Can other players see where I am?

No. Squishrot uses your location only while you play, to place drops around you. Your location is never shown to other players.

How does trading work?

Sign in, list the jellies you want to swap and make offers on other players' listings. Free players can make two offers a week, and Pro unlocks unlimited trading and listings.

Is Squishrot OK for younger players?

Players under their country's age of digital consent play on their phone only, with no account, trading or purchases without a grown-up's check. There's no chat between players, names and listing notes are filtered, and anything can be reported.

How do I cancel Pro?

Pro renews every month unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel it any time in your App Store account settings.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Profile, then Settings, then Delete account. Need a hand? Email support@squishrot.com.
