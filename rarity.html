Rarity & odds

How rare is rare?

Every jelly comes in six finishes, from Common to Diamond. Here are the real odds of finding each one, the same for every player and set by the game’s server.

The odds

What’s inside a drop

Each drop on the map holds one jelly. Its finish is rolled when the drop appears, then one of the 28 jellies is picked, each just as likely as the next.

The rarest finds

One jelly, one finish

Hunting one jelly in one finish? Split its finish’s odds 28 ways. A Diamond of the jelly you want is about 1 in 28,000 drops: the rarest find in Squishrot.

Your chances

How many drops until a Diamond?

Slide to the number of drops you open and see your chance of finding at least one of each finish. You can open up to 40 drops a day.

    Map drops only, at the odds above. Every drop is its own roll, so nothing is ever guaranteed and a lucky find can come on the first drop.

    What a finish is worth

    Rarer jellies earn more

    On your Den’s bed a jelly earns coins every hour, and every jelly you own adds points to your leaderboard score.

    Coins an hour in the Den and leaderboard points for each finish

    Another way up

    Three in, one rarer out

    Put three jellies of one finish in the Blender and out comes one random jelly of the next finish up. Three Shinies always make a Diamond. Favourites stay out of the Blender.

    1. Common
    2. Rare

    6. Diamond
