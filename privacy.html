Privacy Policy

Effective 3 November 2026

Squishrot is a jelly collecting and trading game for iPhone, operated by David Judd. This policy explains what the game collects, why, who helps run it, and how to delete it. If you have a question, email support@squishrot.com.

The short version

  • Squishrot uses your location while the app is open, to put jellies near you. It never tracks you in the background.
  • Your account holds your collection, coins and trades so they're safe if you change phones.
  • Other players see your display name, jelly avatar, collection and trades. They never see your email, location or profile photo.
  • There are no ads, no ad tracking and no analytics or crash-reporting tools in the app.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from Settings.

Players under the age of digital consent

Squishrot asks your age the first time you open it. If you're under the age where your country lets you share personal data online on your own (13 in many countries, up to 16 in parts of Europe), the game plays entirely on your phone:

  • no account, no sign-in and no email address
  • no online trading, leaderboard or coin shop
  • your location stays on your phone and is only used to place jellies there
  • a grown-up check before any purchase, outside link or change of age

The app keeps only whether you're under that age, never your age itself, and only on your phone.

Your account

When you sign in with Apple or with email, our account service receives an account identifier and your email address. If you choose Hide My Email with Sign in with Apple, we only see a private relay address. We use this to sign you in, recover your account and keep your progress attached to it.

You choose a display name, a jelly avatar and a short bio. These are public to other players, along with your collection, your Den, your place on the leaderboard and your trading listings. Active market listings, with the seller's display name, avatar, jellies, asking price and note, can be seen without signing in. Please keep personal contact details out of these fields. Public text is checked by a basic filter for rude words and contact details.

If you pick a profile photo, it stays on your phone. It isn't uploaded, and other players see your jelly avatar instead.

Location

With your permission, Squishrot uses your precise location while the app is open to place jellies near you and to check that you're close enough to open one. Squishrot doesn't ask for background location.

To do this, the app sends your latitude, longitude and location accuracy to our game server. The server keeps your latest location and the places and times of your jelly drops, so it can check that each drop is real. Your location is never shown to other players or included in the market or leaderboard.

To land jellies in places people can walk to, like parks, cafes and stations, the app looks up nearby public places on Apple Maps. Apple receives the area being searched under Apple's privacy policy. The places found are sent to our server along with the drop.

You can turn location off at any time in iPhone Settings. You won't be able to find new jellies on the map without it, but everything else keeps working.

Game progress and purchases

Our server keeps your jellies, their trading history, your coins and diamonds, Den, sets, streak, rewards, blends, listings, offers and finished trades. This keeps your collection in sync and stops jellies or coins being copied.

Apple handles all payments. Our server receives signed purchase details from Apple, like the product, transaction number, purchase date and subscription expiry, and uses them to deliver coins or Squishrot Pro. We never see your card or payment details. If Apple refunds a purchase, Apple tells our server so the game can take the coins or Pro back.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token from Apple with your account, so we can tell you about offers, trades and your Den. We keep a record of these notifications for up to 30 days. You can turn notifications off in iPhone Settings.

Safety reports and support

If you report a player or listing, we keep who sent the report, what was reported, the reason and any details you add, so a person can review it. Blocks are kept so blocked players can't see or trade with you. Reports are private and only used for moderation.

If you email support, we receive your message and anything you choose to include. Please never send passwords, sign-in codes or card details.

Who helps run Squishrot

  • Supabase runs our sign-in service, game database and server functions. See Supabase's privacy policy.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple, App Store payments, Apple Maps and push notifications. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Google Workspace hosts our support mailbox.

They only receive what they need to provide those services. We don't sell your information, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you across other apps and websites.

How long we keep things

  • Your latest location and your opened or expired drops: about one day.
  • Notifications: 30 days.
  • Resolved safety reports: 90 days.
  • Your account and game progress: until you delete your account.

A cleanup job runs every day, so things can stay up to a day longer than these periods. After you delete your account, we keep a closed account number and the coin, purchase and trade records it was part of, so other players' trades and Apple purchases stay accurate. They can't be used to sign in and don't include your email, name, location or profile.

Deleting your account

Open Profile → Settings → Delete account and confirm. This removes your sign-in, email, profile, location, drops, push tokens and game progress, closes your open trades, and retires the jellies you still own. Jellies you already traded to other players stay with them. If you signed in with Apple, the app also asks Apple to disconnect Squishrot from your Apple Account.

Deleting your account or the app doesn't cancel a Squishrot Pro subscription. Cancel it in iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

Your choices

You can change your display name, bio and avatar in the app, turn location and notifications on or off in iPhone Settings, and delete your account in the app. To ask for a copy of your information, a correction or a deletion, email support@squishrot.com. We may need to check the account is yours first. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Squishrot uses your information, we'll update this page and its date, and tell you in the app before an important change takes effect.
