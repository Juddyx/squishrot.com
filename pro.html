What Pro adds

Six perks, every month

Bigger Den & reach

Drops open from 150 m instead of 80 m, so the jelly across the road is yours. At home, your Den’s bed holds four more jellies, each one earning coins every hour.

Daily Pro drop

Every day a Pro drop lands close by on your map with a Rare-or-better jelly inside. Hold it until it pops.

Unlimited trading

Post as many listings and make as many offers as you like, with jellies, coins or both. Free players keep one listing up and make two offers a week.

Streak freeze & rescue

Two freezes a month cover a missed day so your streak carries on, and once a month a rescue brings back a streak of three days or more that broke.

Pro profile

Eight showcase spots instead of four, plus a Pro badge and glow that other players see on your profile and listings.

Jelly of the Month

A Shiny jelly to claim every month, foil and all, just for being Pro.

How Pro works

Simple to start, simple to stop

  1. Get it in the app

    Open Profile and tap Get Pro. Hold the Pro jelly until it pops to see everything you get, then subscribe with your Apple Account.

  2. $4.99 a month

    Pro renews every month through the App Store unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

  3. Cancel any time

    Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Squishrot. Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

  4. New phone?

    Tap Profile → Settings → Restore purchases with the Apple Account you subscribed with.

Pro questions

Before you go Pro

Do I need Pro to play?

No. Squishrot is free to download and play: the map, drops, your Den, the Blender, trading and the leaderboard are all there without Pro. Pro gives you more of each.

How much does Pro cost where I live?

Pro is $4.99 a month in the US. Prices vary by country, and the App Store shows your local price before you subscribe.

When am I charged?

When you subscribe, then every month after that. Pro renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

How do I cancel?

Open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions → Squishrot, or follow Apple’s steps. Pro stays on until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

I paid but Pro isn’t on

Open Squishrot with an internet connection, then tap Profile → Settings → Restore purchases using the Apple Account you subscribed with. Still stuck? Email support@squishrot.com.

Can younger players get Pro?

Players under their country’s age of digital consent need a grown-up’s check before any purchase, Pro included.
